4. QAWAID (rules of logic)
------------------------------------------------------------
Status (core.statusFor): checkIn hai -> late agar (checkIn − shiftStart) > grace, warna present.
  Checkin nahi -> manzoor chutti = leave; weeklyOff = off; AAJ shift+grace se pehle = waiting ("Abhi nahi aaya"); warna absent.
  joinDate se pehle aur aane wale din = na (ginti mein nahi).
Check-out baqi: shiftEnd guzar gaya aur checkOut khali (raat ki shift ke liye agla din). shiftEnd na ho to 24 ghante.
Raat ki shift: openRecord() 16 ghante tak ka khula record deta hai, is liye aadhi raat ke baad bhi Check-Out lagta hai.
Kaam ke minute: checkOut − checkIn, aadhi raat paar theek; 0 ya 16 ghante se zyada = ghalat, nahi gina jata.
Salary (purani app wala formula, badla nahi):
  hourly = monthlySalary / (dutyHours × workingDays)
  har din: normal = min(kaam, dutyHours), overtime = baqi
  final = normal×hourly + OT×(overtimeRate ya hourly) + points×pointRate + bonus + khana(agar mealInSalary) − advance
  mahina "final" ho to snapshot hi dikhta hai (hazri badalne se nahi badalta). Final se pehle koi Check-Out baqi ho to final nahi hota.
  Final mahine mein advance/bonus nahi likha/hataya ja sakta — pehle "Dobara kholein".
  Payment sirf final ke baad, aur baqi se zyada nahi.
Check-In: live selfie + GPS lazmi, dukaan (32.7979125, 73.956984375) se radius (default 200m) ke andar.
  Server 12 s mein jawab na de to "phone mein mehfooz, signal aate hi jayegi" (Firestore persistent cache).
